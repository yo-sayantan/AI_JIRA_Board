What this is

A personal, self-hosted mirror of your Jira board. A background fetcher pulls the tickets assigned to you into a plain data file; a single-file React app renders them — no Jira login, no waiting on the web UI. Two moving parts:

  • The fetch (jira-intern/) — pulls your tickets. Needs one Jira token.
  • The app (dist/index.html) — renders whatever the fetch produced. Needs nothing.
Your tokens never enter git. You add them in the board with the key button in the header; they are saved in one git-ignored file (setup/.env) that is never copied into the Docker image and never served.
